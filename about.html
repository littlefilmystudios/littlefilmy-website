Little Filmy is a boutique entertainment and media studio based in Mumbai, dedicated to advancing the art and craft of purposeful storytelling and compelling advertising. We specialize in television commercials, digital films, animation, short and feature films, offering end-to-end creative and production services under one roof. Whether it's a 30-second spot or a full-fledged brand film, or a 120-min feature film, we bring stories to life with cinematic depth and strategic clarity.
At Little Filmy, creativity meets craft. Our team blends imaginative thinking with sharp execution—pushing the boundaries of storytelling while delivering at the highest visual and operational standards.
