Jayant Raj
Producer & Director

Jayant Raj’s profound passion for storytelling and visual arts has placed him at the forefront of Indian cinema. As a Film maker & an Executive Producer, Jayant seamlessly integrates art, narrative, poetry, music, and performance into his projects, viewing filmmaking as a journey of self-discovery. His work at heart, is an extension of himself. His journey started on the sets of some of the most engaging pieces of storytelling in Indian niche cinema.  Kill, Pagglait, Darkness Visible, Dear Dad, Irada to name a few.
A major part of his career was spent creating internationally acclaimed content. Apart from these, Jayant has helped create several short films such as Finding Nabi, Striking Bonds, Plus Minus, Guddu - Shuruaat Ka Twist, Interior Café Night. Over the years, he has donned several other hats along the way. Producing and post producing for more than 100 commercials in advertising and digital films, Jayant sees technology as a tool to enhance storytelling. His approach is driven by a desire for innovation, striving for a creative process that fosters experimentation and heartfelt artistic expression.
